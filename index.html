Word Counter for Assignment

A free, fast, and privacy-friendly online word counter engineered for students writing essays, research papers, and university assignments. Count words, characters, sentences, paragraphs, reading time, and speaking time in real time with support for English, Urdu, and Roman Urdu.

Key Features:

  • Real-time word, character, and sentence counting with instant typing response
  • Target word limit tracker with dynamic visual progress bar and remaining word alerts
  • Full support for Urdu full stop (۔) and Urdu question mark (؟)
  • Top 5 keyword density analysis excluding common stop words
  • Academic Words to Pages converter for single spacing, double spacing, and handwritten sheets
  • 100% client-side privacy: your assignment drafts never leave your device
