Word Counter for Assignment

Word Counter for Assignment is a free online counter that measures words, characters, sentences, paragraphs, and approximate reading and speaking times right in your browser. Paste or type English, Urdu, or Roman Urdu assignment text to track your target word limit privately with zero uploads or logins.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does this free online counter work?

Paste or type your draft into the editor on this page, and the online counter calculates words, characters with and without spaces, sentences, paragraphs, and approximate reading and speaking times locally inside your browser.

Do in-text citations and reference lists count towards my assignment word limit?

This depends on your university guidelines. Most UK, Australian, and US institutions count in-text citations (such as Harvard or APA author-date citations) in the core word count, but exclude the bibliography or reference list, appendices, and title pages. Always check your module syllabus or rubric.

How does the word counter handle hyphenated words and abbreviations?

Standard academic word counters treat hyphenated compound terms (like "evidence-based" or "state-of-the-art") as single words unless separated by whitespace. Abbreviations with periods (like "e.g." or "i.e.") are treated as individual words without incorrectly splitting your sentences.

Is my assignment text stored or uploaded to any server?

No. Our tool performs all calculations locally in your browser using modern client-side JavaScript. Your text is never transmitted over the internet, never sent to external servers, and never saved in any database, ensuring total academic privacy.
