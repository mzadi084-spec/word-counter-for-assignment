About Word Counter for Assignment

Our Mission

Word Counter for Assignment was built to solve a universal struggle faced by university, college, and secondary school students: submitting coursework that meets strict word limits, page constraints, and formatting requirements without compromising privacy.

Whether you are writing a 1,500-word history essay, converting a 10-page literature review into double-spaced pages, or counting words in an Urdu assignment, our suite of tools delivers instant, reliable feedback directly in your browser.

Engineered for Bilingual & International Students

Most online text analyzers are designed solely for standard English. We recognized that millions of students in Pakistan, India, the UK, the US, and across the globe study and write across multiple languages and writing systems—including English, Urdu, and Roman Urdu.

Our platform natively supports:

  • Urdu Nastaliq Script: Accurate word tokenization and full sentence recognition using authentic Urdu punctuation like the Khatma (۔) and Sawalia Nishan (؟).
  • Roman Urdu: Seamless analysis for students taking lecture notes and drafting ideas in phonetic Latin Urdu.
  • Academic English: Stop-word filtering, keyword density tracking, and speaking/reading duration benchmarks at 200 words per minute.

100% Client-Side Privacy Guarantee

🔒 Your assignment drafts never leave your device. All counting, text parsing, and page estimation happen in real time using local JavaScript inside your browser. No text is ever uploaded to a database, sent to external APIs, or saved on our servers.

Free Forever for Learners Worldwide

Education should be accessible. There are no paywalls, no required registrations, no subscription tiers, and no software installations. Every tool on our website is 100% free for students, teachers, and researchers.
