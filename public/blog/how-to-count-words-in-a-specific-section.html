How to Count Words in a Specific Section of Your Essay

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Quick Answer: Counting One Section

To count words in a specific section, highlight only that section in Microsoft Word or Google Docs to view the selection count, or copy and paste just that passage into a browser word counter. Always check and follow your own course guidelines on excluded parts.

Full-document totals are helpful at the end, yet most academic writing happens section by section. Measuring one part of your essay at a time prevents an oversized introduction or literature review from crowding out your core analysis. You can also explore our assignment word count guide for a broader look at planning and limits.

Why Count One Section at a Time?

Checking a whole paper often mixes counted body paragraphs with title pages, tables of contents, reference lists, and appendices. Measuring one section on its own makes three common coursework tasks much easier:

  • Keeping Your Introduction Concise: Stopping your introduction from drifting past 10 to 15 percent of your essay before your first body argument even begins.
  • Checking Literature Review and Methodology Budgets: Research briefs and lab reports frequently set separate target ranges for methods, results, and discussion.
  • Balancing Body Arguments: Ensuring one case study does not consume twice as many words as two equally weighted points.

Counting Selected Text in Word and Google Docs

Both major word processors let you check a single passage without moving anything out of your file:

  1. Click at the start of the section you want to check and drag to highlight down to the end of that section.
  2. In most versions of Microsoft Word: Look at the bottom-left status bar. Instead of showing only the document total, it switches to a format such as 342 of 1,850 words, or click Review > Word Count to view characters and paragraphs for the selected text.
  3. In most versions of Google Docs: Press Ctrl + Shift + C (Windows/Chromebook) or Command + Shift + C (Mac), or open Tools > Word count. The pop-up shows the highlighted words alongside the document total, and if you turned on the live counter badge, it displays 342 of 1,850 words automatically.

Section Word Budget Example: 1,500-Word Essay

Many students find it easier to hit a 1,500-word target by splitting the essay into five clear parts rather than writing toward one large number:

Essay Section Approximate Word Budget Share of Essay
Introduction About 150 words About 10%
Body Section 1 About 400 words About 27%
Body Section 2 About 400 words About 27%
Body Section 3 About 400 words About 27%
Conclusion About 150 words About 10%

These numbers are helpful planning targets rather than rigid academic rules. Always follow your own course guidelines if your lecturer specifies a different balance between sections.

How to Count Without References, Headings, or Long Quotes

When an assignment brief excludes bibliographies, section headings, or appendices, full-document counters quickly give a misleading total. Follow this simple workflow:

  • Exclude References and Appendices: Highlight from the start of your introduction down to the final period of your conclusion, stopping right above your References or Bibliography page.
  • Exclude Non-Counted Headings or Tables: Copy your counted prose into our main assignment word counter and delete any uncounted subheadings, figure captions, or excluded appendices inside the box so only counted text remains.
  • Check Block Quotations: If your brief counts block quotes normally, leave them in; if your brief sets a separate limit on quoted material, measure those quotes separately so you know both totals.

If checking each section reveals that one part runs well above budget, use the editing techniques in our guide on how to reduce word count in an assignment to tighten filler phrases without cutting your analysis.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I count words in one paragraph?

Highlight just that paragraph in Microsoft Word or Google Docs to see the selection word count, or copy and paste that single paragraph into a browser word counter or paragraph counter to inspect its word and sentence totals.

Does word count include headings?

Word processors include any heading inside your selected text. Whether section headings count toward your assignment limit depends on your course guidelines—some briefs count them while others exclude headings—so copy only the text your brief includes.

How do I count words without references?

Select from the first word of your introduction down to the last word of your conclusion, stopping just before your References or Bibliography heading. Check the selection total in your editor or paste only that highlighted portion into a browser word counter.

How many words should an essay introduction be?

As a common rule of thumb, an essay introduction often takes about 10 percent of the total word count—for instance, about 150 words in a 1,500-word essay—though that is a helpful guideline rather than a strict rule. Always follow your own course brief.
