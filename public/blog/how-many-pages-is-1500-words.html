How Many Pages Is 1,500 Words? Single & Double Spaced

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Quick Answer: 1,500 Words in Pages

Using about 500 words per single-spaced page and about 250 words per double-spaced page (12-point Times New Roman, 1-inch margins), 1,500 words is about 3 pages single-spaced and about 5.5 to 6 pages double-spaced. Handwritten on lined A4 paper, 1,500 words spans roughly 8.5 to 9 pages. Follow your own syllabus rules.

Undergraduate courses frequently assign 1,500-word essays as mid-semester assessments, critical literature responses, and case study analyses. Knowing both page conversion estimates and section pacing keeps your research tight and cohesive.

Page Estimates for 1,500 Words by Formatting

The page total for 1,500 words changes predictably based on line spacing, font choice, and handwriting size:

1.5 Spacing About 330 to 350 words About 4.3 to 4.5 pages

Why Real 1,500-Word Essays Often Pass 6 Pages

Even when an instructor specifies double spacing and 12-point font, three standard academic elements push 1,500 words slightly past the sixth sheet:

  • Cover Pages & Reference Lists: Your main text takes about 6 double-spaced pages; adding a separate APA title page and a one-page bibliography yields 8 physical pages in your exported PDF.
  • Section Headings & Subheadings: Management, nursing, and engineering reports split arguments into titled sections, adding spacing above and below headings. Read whether headings count toward word limits before counting.
  • Short Block Quotations & Tables: Indented 40-word quotes or small data tables compress line width and increase total printed length.

Sample 1,500-Word Essay Plan (10-80-10 Ratio)

Following a balanced 10% introduction, 80% body, and 10% conclusion breakdown prevents spending too many words summarizing background readings before reaching analysis:

Essay Section Target Word Allocation Paragraph Guide
Introduction About 150 words 1 focused paragraph ending with thesis statement.
Body Argument 1 About 380 to 400 words 2 analytical paragraphs with peer-reviewed evidence.
Body Argument 2 About 380 to 400 words 2 paragraphs analyzing second theme or case data.
Body Argument 3 / Counterpoint About 380 to 400 words 2 paragraphs evaluating limitations and opposing views.
Conclusion About 150 words 1 synthesizing paragraph connecting findings to thesis.

How Long Does 1,500 Words Take to Write & Read?

Reading 1,500 words silently takes roughly 7.5 minutes at 200 words per minute, while delivering 1,500 words aloud at ~130 words per minute requires around 11.5 minutes. Drafting a well-cited 1,500-word essay—excluding initial library research—commonly takes three to five hours across outlining, drafting, and citation verification. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How many pages is 1,500 words double-spaced?

Using standard 12-point Times New Roman with 1-inch margins and double spacing, 1,500 words is about 5.5 to 6 pages.

How many pages is 1,500 words single-spaced?

Single-spaced documents hold about 500 words per page, making 1,500 words approximately 3 pages.

How many paragraphs is a 1,500-word assignment?

A 1,500-word essay typically has about 8 to 10 paragraphs: one introduction paragraph, six to eight body paragraphs of roughly 150 to 200 words each, and one conclusion paragraph.

What is the ±10% safe range for a 1,500-word paper?

Where universities allow a ±10% margin, a 1,500-word assignment generally accepts about 1,350 to 1,650 words. Always confirm with your course brief.
