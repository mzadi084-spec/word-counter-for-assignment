How to Check Word Count in a Document (Word, Docs, Pages)

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Quick Answer: Checking Document Word Count

To check word count in a document, use the bottom status bar or Word Count tool in Microsoft Word, Tools > Word count in Google Docs, View > Show Word Count in Apple Pages, or paste your text into a browser counter. Always follow your own course guidelines.

Whether you are revising a short reflection or checking a multi-page essay before submitting, knowing where each editor shows its totals keeps your draft aligned with your rubric. Before you check your total, review our assignment word count guide to confirm which parts of your paper belong inside the limit.

How to Check Word Count in Microsoft Word

In most versions of Microsoft Word on Windows and macOS, your total word count sits in the status bar along the bottom-left edge of the window. Clicking that number opens the Word Count dialog box, or you can open it from the Review tab by selecting Word Count.

That dialog box shows pages, words, characters with and without spaces, paragraphs, and lines. Look closely at the checkbox labeled Include textboxes, footnotes and endnotes: turning it on or off can shift your word count noticeably if your essay includes explanatory footnotes, callout boxes, or captions. If your assignment guidelines exclude footnotes or references, uncheck that option or highlight only your main essay text before checking the count.

If the word count is missing from your bottom status bar in desktop Word, right-click anywhere on the status bar and confirm that Word Count is checked.

How to Check Word Count in Google Docs

In most versions of Google Docs on the web, open Tools > Word count from the top menu, or press Ctrl + Shift + C (Windows/Chromebook) or Command + Shift + C (Mac). A pop-up window displays pages, words, characters, and characters excluding spaces.

At the bottom of that pop-up window, check the box labeled Display word count while typing. A small floating badge will stay in the bottom-left corner of your document so you can monitor your progress live without reopening the menu. You can also click that badge to switch between words, characters, and pages.

How to Check Word Count in Apple Pages

In most versions of Apple Pages on Mac, click View in the top menu bar and select Show Word Count (or press Shift + Command + W). A small counter pill appears near the bottom of your canvas showing your current word count.

Clicking the arrows on that counter lets you switch between words, characters with or without spaces, paragraphs, and pages. When you highlight a single section, Pages updates that counter to show only the selected passage.

How to Check Word Count on a Phone

Students frequently check final totals or quick edits on a phone before uploading. In most mobile versions of Microsoft Word and Google Docs, tap the edit pencil icon first if the document is in read-only mode, then tap the three-dot menu in the upper corner and choose Word count (in Google Docs) or switch to the Review tab and tap Word Count (in Microsoft Word).

If you are checking a PDF, plain text note, email draft, or an assignment saved in an app without a built-in counter, select the text you want to measure, copy it, and paste it into a mobile browser counter.

Why Word Counts Differ Slightly Between Programs

Do not be surprised if Microsoft Word, Google Docs, your university upload portal, and an online tool disagree by a few words on a long essay. Minor differences usually come from a handful of formatting rules:

  • Hyphenated Words and Em Dashes: One tool may count decision-making as one word while another splits it into two, especially around long dashes without spaces.
  • Footnotes, Captions, and Text Boxes: Desktop Word can include or exclude footnotes and text boxes depending on a checkbox, whereas Google Docs handles footnotes separately in some views.
  • Numbers, Symbols, and Punctuation: Standalone dashes, bullet symbols, or decimal numbers such as 3.5 or 1,000 are tokenized slightly differently by older word processors—and if your draft includes Urdu script or mixed Urdu-English terms, see our guide to counting words in Urdu text.
  • Headers, Footers, and References: Full-document counts include your title block and bibliography unless you select only the body text, which we explain in our guide on how to count words in a specific section.

For a closer look at how universities handle those edge cases—including in-text citations, tables, and allowed buffers—check our guide to university assignment word count guidelines, and always follow your own course guidelines as the final standard. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I check word count in Microsoft Word?

In most versions of Microsoft Word, look at the status bar in the bottom-left corner to see your live word count, or click Review and choose Word Count to view characters, paragraphs, lines, and whether text boxes, footnotes, and endnotes are included.

How do I check word count in Google Docs?

In most versions of Google Docs, open Tools and select Word count, or press Ctrl + Shift + C on Windows and Command + Shift + C on Mac. You can also turn on Display word count while typing so a small box stays visible.

Does Word count references in the word count?

Yes, a full-document count in Word or Google Docs includes your reference list unless you select only the main body text first. Always check your course guidelines to confirm whether references or appendices count toward your assignment limit.

How do I check word count on my phone?

Mobile Word and Google Docs apps usually list Word count inside the top-right three-dot menu or Review tab. You can also select your text, copy it, and paste it into a browser-based word counter to check words, characters, and paragraphs without installing anything.
