Reporting a Bug or Count Discrepancy

If you encounter an unexpected word or character count with a specific language, script, or punctuation mark, please let us know! In your email, include:

  • A brief snippet of the sample text causing the discrepancy
  • The browser and operating system you are using (e.g. Chrome on Windows, Safari on Mac)
  • The expected count compared to what our tool displayed
