What You Can Contact Us About

Whether you use our English assignment word counter, Urdu word counter, paragraph counter, character counter, or words to pages converter, feel free to reach out about any of the following:

  • Reporting a bug or count discrepancy with English, Urdu punctuation (۔ or ؟), or Roman Urdu text

  • Questions about how our browser-only Privacy Policy or Terms of Service work

Helpful Details for Bug Reports

Please do not attach confidential assignments or personal documents. Instead, if you are reporting a counting issue, include only a short non-sensitive example:

  • The page URL where the issue occurred (for example, the main counter or words to pages converter)
  • A brief sample string showing the punctuation or spacing case you tested
  • Your browser and device type (such as Chrome on Windows or Safari on mobile)

Maintainer & Common Support Notes

Word Counter for Assignment is independently maintained by Farwa Naveed (learn more on our About Us page). Before sending an email, note these quick answers to common support questions:

  • Differences from Microsoft Word or Turnitin: Word processors handle hyphenated terms, footnotes, and text boxes differently. Always check your course rubric and copy only the body text required by your instructor.
  • Planning estimates: Reading speeds (~200 wpm), speaking speeds (~130 wpm), and page conversions (~500 single-spaced, ~250 double-spaced, and ~175 handwritten words per page) are general guides only and vary by font, spacing, and speaker.
  • Draft recovery: Because every tool runs 100% locally in your browser memory with zero server uploads, we cannot recover unsaved text after a tab closes or refreshes.
