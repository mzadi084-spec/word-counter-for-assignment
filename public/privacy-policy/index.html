Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 22, 2026
Website: https://wordcounterforassignment.com

Client-Side Processing: Text you type or paste and PDF files you select on Word Counter for Assignment are computed locally in your web browser via JavaScript and PDF.js, without an application draft-upload API or user database storing your documents.

1. Text Content Privacy (Client-Side Processing)

We respect the confidentiality of your academic work. When you use any of our counting tools (including the Main Word Counter, Urdu Word Counter, Roman Urdu Word Counter, Words to Pages Converter, Character Counter, Paragraph Counter, Sentence Counter, Line Counter, Unique Word Counter, or Calculators), all calculations take place locally inside your computer or mobile device's browser memory.

We do not operate backend databases or server-side APIs that receive, log, parse, or store the contents of your assignments, essays, or personal writings.

2. Client-Side PDF Processing

Our PDF Word Counter parses selectable text layers directly on your device using browser-side PDF.js, parsed locally inside browser memory without submitting your PDF to a conversion backend.

3. Cookies, Advertising & Third-Party Services

If Google AdSense or similar third-party advertising networks are enabled in the future to support free educational access, those third-party vendors may use cookies or standard web identifiers to serve relevant ads in accordance with Google’s advertising policies. You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time without impacting core word counting features.

4. Standard Hosting Logs & External Links

Like standard static websites, routine server logs may record technical request metadata (such as requested page path, timestamp, and browser type) strictly for security and uptime monitoring. None of the text you enter in any counter or upload to the PDF tool is included in those requests.

Our website and blog articles may contain links to university style guides, academic writing resources, and citation manuals. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party external websites.

5. Children's Online Privacy

Word Counter for Assignment is intended for general student and educational audiences. We do not require accounts or knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
