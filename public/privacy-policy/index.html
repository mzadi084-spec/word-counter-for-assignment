Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Website: https://wordcounterforassignment.com

🔒 Zero Server Processing: Text you type or paste into Word Counter for Assignment is processed exclusively in your web browser using local JavaScript. Your assignment drafts, essays, and notes are never stored, sent to any server, or accessible to us or any third party.

1. Text Content Privacy (Client-Side Processing)

We respect the confidentiality of your academic work. When you use any of our counting tools (including the Main Word Counter, Urdu Word Counter, Words to Pages Converter, or Character Counter), all calculations take place locally inside your computer or mobile device's browser memory.

We do not operate backend servers that receive, log, parse, or store the contents of your assignments, essays, or personal writings.

2. Cookies, Analytics & Google Search Console

To understand overall visitor trends and maintain website performance, we may use standard web tools such as Google Search Console and privacy-conscious analytics services. These tools record non-personally identifiable technical information such as:

  • Browser type, screen resolution, and operating system
  • Referring websites and entry/exit pages
  • General geographical region (country or city level, never precise street address)
  • Site speed, page load errors, and crawl indexing statistics

You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser settings at any time without impacting the functionality of our word counters.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdSense)

To keep Word Counter for Assignment 100% free for students worldwide, we may partner with third-party advertising networks like Google AdSense to display advertisements when you visit our website. These advertising companies may use cookies and web beacons to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings (https://adssettings.google.com) or via the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page.

4. External Links

Our website and blog articles may contain informational links to university style guides, academic writing resources, and citation manuals. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party external websites.

5. Children's Online Privacy

Word Counter for Assignment is intended for general student and educational audiences. We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

6. Contact Regarding Privacy

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our team at contact@wordcounterforassignment.com.
