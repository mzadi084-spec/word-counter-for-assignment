Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 14, 2026
Website: https://wordcounterforassignment.com

Zero Server Processing: Text you type or paste into Word Counter for Assignment is processed exclusively in your web browser using local JavaScript. Your assignment drafts, essays, and notes are never stored, sent to any server, or accessible to us or any third party.

1. Text Content Privacy (Client-Side Processing)

We respect the confidentiality of your academic work. When you use any of our counting tools (including the Main Word Counter, Urdu Word Counter, Words to Pages Converter, Character Counter, or Paragraph Counter), all calculations take place locally inside your computer or mobile device's browser memory.

We do not operate backend databases or server-side APIs that receive, log, parse, or store the contents of your assignments, essays, or personal writings.

2. Local Browser Storage & No Tracking Ads

We do not run behavioral ad networks, advertising trackers, or analytics scripts that monitor what you type into our tools. Your browser may remember basic display settings locally so pages load cleanly across visits. You can clear site storage or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time without impacting core word counting features.

3. Standard Hosting Logs & External Links

Like standard static websites, routine server logs may record technical request metadata (such as requested page path, timestamp, and browser type) strictly for security and uptime monitoring. None of the text you enter in any counter is included in those requests.

Our website and blog articles may contain links to university style guides, academic writing resources, and citation manuals. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party external websites.

4. Children's Online Privacy

Word Counter for Assignment is intended for general student and educational audiences. We do not require accounts or knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

5. Contact Regarding Privacy

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at mzadi084@gmail.com.
